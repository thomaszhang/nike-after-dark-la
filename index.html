Before your run
  1. Live tracking starts automatically. Allow precise location when Safari asks.
  2. Keep this page open while running. Add it to your Home Screen for one-tap access.
  3. The blue dot is you. Distance, remaining distance, elevation, and status sit above the bottom profile.
  4. The top navigation card combines your next course turn and live heading direction.
  5. The square card cycles Route overview → Location north-up → Location heading-up. Dragging the map pauses centering; tap to recenter the selected mode.
  6. Rotate the map manually with two fingers. Manual rotation turns Direction off.
  7. Heading-up also follows the course bearing while previewing the profile. Releasing restores your previous view.

iPhone may pause browser location after the screen locks. This page requests that the screen stay awake when supported.
